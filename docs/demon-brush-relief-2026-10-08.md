# Demon plaques on moving brush entities get their relief too

Owner report (8 Oct 2026, with a screenshot): a horned demon face "does not have the displacement map the
same as the other faces do". See `docs/demon-displacement-2026-10-03.md` for the relief itself.

## Cause

The relief meshes are built from the **world model's** surfaces only (`gl_rsurf.js`, `demonSurfaces`, filled
while the world batches are built, with the world's PVS). A demon plaque on a **brush entity** (a door, a
plat, a secret wall) is not in that list, so its face stayed the flat native polygon, however its texture and
normal map looked.

The stock levels have exactly one such case: **e1m4, inline model `*44`**, a `func_door` (`targetname t76`,
`angle -1`, opens upward, `wait -1`) that is a 64 x 128 x 8 slab with `dem5_3` on all six faces, so a face
that shows flat among the world's relief faces (`R_DemonReliefStatus().eligible` is 5 there: the world's five
plaques; the door's are not counted). Every other plaque of the shareware levels is in the world model (a
BSP scan of `pak0.pak` lists them). Brush-entity plaques in other maps (the full game, add-ons, custom maps)
had the same gap and are covered now.

## Fix

`gl_rsurf.js` `R_UpdateBrushDemon`, called from `R_DrawBrushModel` for the entity's cached group:

* the entity's demon-textured surfaces are found once (`R_BrushDemonCandidates`); only the **large faces** are
  raised (texture-coordinate span of at least half a tile on both axes: the two 64 x 128 plaques of the door;
  the 8-unit edges, which would otherwise grow bumps along a slab's edge, stay flat);
* once the sculpted height has loaded (`texture.gl_texture.userData.newerHeight`, asynchronous) the face is
  generated by the same `R_DemonSurfaceData` as the world's (the prepared bakes are keyed to the world model),
  built with the world's displaced material and the entity's own lightmap, and added as a **child of the
  entity's group**, so it moves with the door (position and rotation) and is drawn when the entity is;
* the entity's own flat face stays as its backing, and the mesh is tagged Newer-only (the classic half of the
  title demo and Classic Quake show the flat face), hidden with `r_newer_normals 0` / `r_newer_textures 0`;
* it is disposed with its group: when the group is rebuilt (texture animation changes the frame) the relief is
  made again, and on a new level the groups, their reliefs' materials and the candidates are released.

Not done (limits): the world's plaques are in the sun's shadow caster, the door's relief is not (the caster is
built once from the world and the door moves); the relief is generated at run time on first draw (two
faces of about 133,000 triangles each, measured at about 65 ms each in Node on this machine, so a one-off
hitch of roughly 130 ms the first time the door is seen), not from the prepared bakes.

## Verification

* `tests/demon_brush_test.js` (4), on the real e1m4 level and its inline models: the door is `*44` with six
  `dem5_3` faces; no relief before the height arrives; with it, exactly two plaque meshes (not the four
  edges), children of the entity's group, equal to the world generator's output for those faces, nothing
  rebuilt on later draws; they follow the door when it moves; hidden by the normals and textures options and by
  Classic and tagged Newer-only; a frame change disposes the old geometries and materials and rebuilds; an
  ordinary brush entity gets nothing; the world still has its five plaques; a new level disposes the entity
  relief's materials and forgets its candidates. Five deliberate mutations (edges raised, relief not a child of
  the group, a leaked material, stale candidates after a rebuild, not hidden when disabled) each make a test
  fail. The existing demon tests (9 files) still pass.
* Real browser (Chromium on Metal, e1m4, the door's face from the front): before, a flat plaque with its
  painted shading; after, the same face as relief (horns, brow, cheeks, mouth stand out), with two relief
  meshes in the scene.
