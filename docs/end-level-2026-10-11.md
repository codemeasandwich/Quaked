# The last level: Shub-Niggurath's spiked ball, the final teleporter and the hub wall

Card [END1]. Owner report, 11 October 2026, on `end.bsp` (the registered game; open it with `?game=quake`):

- the spiked ball that starts when you cross into the arena should move cleanly and fluidly, do a lap of the level and
  pass into the back of the boss;
- the teleporter at the end should send you to exactly where the ball is, and show a live, continually updating view
  from the ball;
- a large wall showed the introductory hub.

Branch `claude-end-level` from Dev `cf3987a6`.

## The level, as the map and QuakeC have it

The figures come from the 2021 pak, read with a scratch parser.

- **The ball.** `misc_teleporttrain` (`progs/teleport.mdl`, targetname `t33`) is a MOVETYPE_PUSH train.
  - Its QuakeC gives it speed 200 and angular velocity `'100 200 300'`.
  - Its lap runs over 30 `path_corner`s. None of them has a `wait`.
  - On the leg from t20 to t21 it passes through Shub-Niggurath (`monster_oldone`, health 40000) from behind.
- **What starts it.** `trigger_once *4` starts the ball when you cross it.
- **The final teleporter.** `trigger_teleport *3` (spawnflags 2049: players only, not in deathmatch) targets `t33`,
  the ball itself.
  - `teleport_touch` puts you at the ball's origin and drops a `spawn_tdeath` there.
  - Arriving while the ball is inside Shub telefrags him.
- **Deathmatch versions.** `*41` is a deathmatch-only twin of `*3` (spawnflags 1792), with a fixed destination.
  - `*40` is a deathmatch-only `trigger_changelevel` to `start`, hanging 447 units above the arena floor.

## What was wrong, and what changed

### 1. The ball never moved, so the game could not be finished (base engine, Classic too)

- **Cause.** `SV_Physics_Pusher` (`src/engine/server/sv_phys.js`) sent any pusher with angular velocity to
  `SV_PushRotate`. That function only turns the angles: this is WinQuake's `QUAKE2` branch, added with
  `SV_PushRotate` in 59283ddb.
- **Effect.** The train has both velocity and spin, so it turned in place, and `SUB_CalcMoveDone`'s `setorigin`
  jumped it from corner to corner. No corner is inside Shub, so he could never be telefragged.
- **Fix.** A pusher that moves now goes by `SV_PushMove`, as in WinQuake, and keeps its spin (angles += avelocity
  × the time moved). Only a pusher turning in place still uses `SV_PushRotate`.
- **Classic too.** This restores the original game, so the change is in the base engine.

### 2. The ball's lap is fluid (Newer Game)

- **Stock behaviour.** Stock QuakeC `train_wait` stops a train for 0.1 s at every corner, even one without a
  `wait`, so the ball halted 19 times a lap.
- **Fix.** `src/newer/gameplay/sv_teleporttrain.js` runs after a pusher's think (hook `SV_TeleportTrainThought`).
  When a teleport train has just been left 0.1 s from `train_next` at a corner with no wait, it runs `train_next`
  at once.
- **What stays the same.** The path, the speed and the corners are the level's own. Other trains, doors and
  platforms are unchanged. Classic keeps the stops.

### 3. The final teleporter's window follows the ball (Newer Game)

- **Before.** Live portals (`src/newer/render/gl_portal.js`) accepted only `info_teleport_destination` targets.
  `*3`'s faces went to `*41` (the deathmatch twin) and to `*39` (a large post-death teleporter), and showed a fixed
  view of Shub.
- **The window.** `R_BuildPortals` now builds a following window for a teleporter whose target is a
  `misc_teleporttrain`:
  - Its receiver is the train's own origin; no 27 is added, because `teleport_touch` puts you there.
  - It faces yaw 0, since the train has no `mangle`.
- **Every frame.** `R_PortalsBeginFrame` moves the receiver to where the ball's client entity is drawn this frame
  (`followTrain`). It rebuilds the view matrix. When the ball enters another leaf, it refreshes the window's
  visibility and the server's link through it (`portalSight`).
- **From inside the ball.** The ball's own model is hidden while its view is drawn.
- **Left out triggers.** A trigger the game leaves out at this skill or in this kind of game gets no window: not
  for deathmatch in single player, `*41`; and in start.bsp `*45` and `*49`.
- **Ties.** Between equally near triggers, one that always works now wins over one with a targetname, which only
  works for 0.2 s after the level uses it (`*39`).
- **Unchanged.** The teleport itself is QuakeC's own, so the telefrag happens at the ball's origin. `sv_portal.js`
  still accepts only `info_teleport_destination` receivers for walk-through travel.

### 4. No start-level picture on the wall behind Shub (Newer Game)

- **Before.** Seamless travel made `*40`, the deathmatch exit hanging 447 units up, into a crossing. Its doorway
  dropped to the arena floor and widened to the open arena, so the start level showed across the wall behind Shub.
  Walking through it did nothing.
- **Fix.** `SV_SeamlessSetup` (`src/newer/gameplay/sv_seamless.js`) now leaves a plane exit floating more than 128
  units above the floor below it to the game.
- **The check.** A scan of every id1 level exit found all the others at most 39 units above their floor. The
  slipgate's small view window to `start` (`*47`) remains.

## Evidence

- **`tests/end_level_native_test.js`.** The real end.bsp and progs are read from the owner's local registered pak;
  the test is a named skip without it.
  - The ball is started by its own `use` and moved by the real `SV_Physics_Pusher`. In Classic and Newer Game it
    flies a lap at its speed with no jump larger than one frame's travel, spins, and passes through Shub.
  - Newer Game is never still; Classic has stock Quake's corner stops.
  - In both modes, the final teleporter's own `teleport_touch`, while the ball is inside Shub, telefrags him.
  - Seamless travel on end.bsp keeps only the slipgate's window.
  - Deliberate breaks were caught:
    - the old pusher branch fails all four ball tests;
    - removing the corner hook fails the Newer Game test;
    - a limitless floating-exit rule fails the wall test.
- **`tests/gl_portal_test.js`.**
  - The window onto a train: the receiver is the train's origin, it follows the ball's current entity (not a stale
    one), and the ball is hidden only during its own view.
  - The deathmatch twin is left out in single player and kept in deathmatch.
  - Each deliberate break was caught: no following, no inhibition, the ball not hidden, +27, a stale entity.
- **`tests/sv_portal_hull_test.js`.** start.bsp's deathmatch-only teleporters no longer get single-player windows
  (13 reachable windows become 11).
- **Browser.** Newer Game, `map end`:
  - no hub picture on the wall behind Shub;
  - the final teleporter shows a changing live view from the ball (four close-ups, one second apart, as the ball
    flew round the arena);
  - the ball flew with no jumps;
  - stepping into the teleporter while the ball was inside Shub took his health from 40000 to -99.
