# The last level: Shub-Niggurath's spiked ball, the final teleporter and the hub wall

Card [END1]. Owner report, 11 October 2026, on `end.bsp` (the registered game; open it with `?game=quake`):

- the spiked ball that starts when you cross into the arena should move cleanly and fluidly, do a lap of the level and
  pass into the back of the boss;
- the teleporter at the end should send you to exactly where the ball is, and show a live, continually updating view
  from the ball;
- a large wall showed the introductory hub.

Branch `claude-end-level` from Dev `cf3987a6`.

## The level, as the map and QuakeC have it

The figures come from the 2021 pak, read with a scratch parser.

- **The ball.** `misc_teleporttrain` (`progs/teleport.mdl`, targetname `t33`) is a MOVETYPE_PUSH train.
  - The map gives it speed 200 (QuakeC's default is 100), and its QuakeC gives it angular velocity `'100 200 300'`.
  - Its lap runs over 30 `path_corner`s. None of them has a `wait`.
  - On the leg from t20 to t21 it passes through Shub-Niggurath (`monster_oldone`, health 40000) from behind.
- **What starts it.** `trigger_once *4` starts the ball when you cross it.
- **The final teleporter.** `trigger_teleport *3` (spawnflags 2049: players only, not in deathmatch) targets `t33`,
  the ball itself.
  - `teleport_touch` puts you at the ball's origin and drops a `spawn_tdeath` there.
  - Arriving while the ball is inside Shub telefrags him.
- **Deathmatch versions.** `*41` is a deathmatch-only twin of `*3` (spawnflags 1792), with a fixed destination.
- **The floating exit.** `*40` is a `trigger_changelevel` to `start` hanging 447 units above the arena floor. It has no
  spawnflags, so it exists in every mode. Its message describes it as the way out in deathmatch.

## What was wrong, and what changed

### 1. The ball never moved, so the game could not be finished (base engine, Classic too)

- **Cause.** `SV_Physics_Pusher` (`src/engine/server/sv_phys.js`) sent any pusher with angular velocity to
  `SV_PushRotate`. That function only turns the angles: this is WinQuake's `QUAKE2` branch, added with
  `SV_PushRotate` in 59283ddb.
- **Effect.** The train has both velocity and spin, so it turned in place, and `SUB_CalcMoveDone`'s `setorigin`
  jumped it from corner to corner. No corner is inside Shub, so he could never be telefragged.
- **Fix.** A pusher that moves now goes by `SV_PushMove`, as WinQuake always did. Only a pusher turning in place
  still uses `SV_PushRotate`.
- **Classic too.** This restores the original game's movement, so the change is in the base engine.
- **The spin.** WinQuake never turned a moving pusher, and `progs/teleport.mdl` has no rotate flag, so the original
  ball does not spin. Classic keeps that: there, only a brush pusher turns in place (`SV_PushRotate`, as this port
  already did for rotating doors and platforms). The ball is an alias model, not solid, so turning it pushes nothing.
- **Newer Game spin.** The ball spins as it flies (angles += avelocity × the time moved, only when it really moved)
  and while it waits. The spin is a picture: it follows Newer Game alone, also in co-op or deathmatch, where the
  corner stops (a gameplay rule) stay.

### 2. The ball's lap is fluid (Newer Game)

- **Stock behaviour.** Stock QuakeC `train_wait` stops a train for 0.1 s at every corner, even one without a
  `wait`, so the ball halted at each of the 30 corners of its lap.
- **Fix.** `src/newer/gameplay/sv_teleporttrain.js` runs after a pusher's think (hook `SV_TeleportTrainThought`).
  When a teleport train has just been left 0.1 s from `train_next` at a corner with no wait, it runs `train_next`
  at once.
- **What stays the same.** The path, the speed and the corners are the level's own. Other trains, doors and
  platforms are unchanged (a `func_train` on the same path keeps its stops). Classic keeps the stops.
- **Where it applies.** As the other Newer gameplay rules, it applies to a local single-player game (not dedicated,
  not a demo) on the stock progs.

### 3. The final teleporter's window follows the ball (Newer Game)

- **Before.** Live portals (`src/newer/render/gl_portal.js`) accepted only `info_teleport_destination` targets.
  `*3`'s faces went to `*41` (the deathmatch twin) and to `*39` (a large post-death teleporter), and showed a fixed
  view of Shub.
- **The window.** `R_BuildPortals` now builds a following window for a teleporter whose target is a
  `misc_teleporttrain`:
  - Its receiver is the train's own origin; no 27 is added, because `teleport_touch` puts you there. Until the
    ball's entity first arrives, the receiver is where the map places it.
  - It faces yaw 0, since the train has no `mangle`.
- **Every frame.** `R_PortalsBeginFrame` moves the receiver to where the ball's client entity is drawn this frame
  (`followTrain`), reusing its arrays, and rebuilds the view matrix. When the ball enters another leaf
  (`portalSight`), it does three things:
  - it refreshes the window's visibility and the server's link through it;
  - it makes the receiver's leaf set be built again;
  - it bumps `R_PortalsVisRevision`, so `R_MarkLeaves` (`gl_rsurf_batches.js`) marks what the window now sees
    although the viewer has not moved. Without that, the [END1] review found the window's world going missing while
    the player stood still.
- **From inside the ball.** The ball's own model is hidden while its view is drawn.
- **Left out triggers.** A trigger the game leaves out gets no window.
  - In deathmatch that is one marked not for deathmatch. The game type comes from the server's `svc_serverinfo`, so a
    local-play window, a page of its own, agrees with its host.
  - Otherwise it is one marked not for any skill (1792): a deathmatch-only trigger. In single player that removes the
    windows of end `*41`, start `*49`, e1m3 `*93` and e3m5 `*55`.
  - The skill itself is not sent to clients, and no id1 teleporter is marked for some skills only.
- **Ties.** Between equally near triggers, one that always works now wins over one with a targetname, which only
  works for 0.2 s after the level uses it (`*39`).
- **Unchanged.** The teleport itself is QuakeC's own, so the telefrag happens at the ball's origin. `sv_portal.js`
  still accepts only `info_teleport_destination` receivers for walk-through travel.

### 4. No start-level picture on the wall behind Shub (Newer Game)

- **Before.** Seamless travel made `*40`, the deathmatch exit hanging 447 units up, into a crossing. Its doorway
  dropped to the arena floor and widened to the open arena, so the start level showed across the wall behind Shub.
  Walking through it did nothing.
- **Fix.** `SV_SeamlessSetup` (`src/newer/gameplay/sv_seamless.js`) now leaves a plane exit floating more than 128
  units above the floor below it to the game.
- **The check.** A scan of every id1 level exit found all the others at most 39 units above their floor. The
  slipgate's small view window to `start` (`*47`) remains.

## Follow-up (card [END2], owner report 11 October 2026)

### The secret teleporter behind the Quad Damage

- **The teleporter.** On a ledge past the final teleporter, behind the Quad Damage at
  (-624, 2672, 120), is a secret teleporter: trigger `*58` (always on, not in deathmatch) to t67.
- **Why it had no window.** Its face is on a `func_wall` (`*59`), not the world. The window
  builder looked only at the world's faces. The renderer also never drew a brush entity's
  liquid and teleporter faces at all, so the face was simply missing.
- **The builder now** also takes the `*teleport` faces of the level's static walls
  (`func_wall`, `func_illusionary`, without an origin, not left out by skill or game type).
- **Brush entities now draw their liquid faces** (`gl_rsurf_brush.js`): water, slime, lava and
  teleporter faces, warped each frame as the world's are, in the entity's own group. WinQuake
  draws them, so this applies in Classic too. A face that leads somewhere is a window. A face's
  mesh belongs to its surface, so a rebuilt group does not throw it away.
- **Another map changes.** The only other id1 brush entity with such a face is E1M3's `*98`,
  whose teleporter face was also invisible and now shows.
- **Which teleporter gets a face.** An always-on teleporter now takes any face within reach
  over one with a targetname, however near that one is. End.bsp's large post-death teleporter
  `*39` had taken the back face of the secret teleporter and the back faces of the final
  doorway; those now show the secret teleporter's and the ball's views.
- **Every map checked.** Rebuilding the windows of all 32 id1 maps before and after, only
  end.bsp's windows changed.

### The flashlight in cutscenes

- **The report.** The finale (Shub-Niggurath blowing up, the player standing in the middle)
  showed the flashlight's beam.
- **The fix.** The beam is now out whenever the client is in an intermission:
  - the end-of-level view (`svc_intermission`);
  - the finale (`svc_finale`);
  - a cutscene (`svc_cutscene`: the mission packs' end scenes).
- **The setting is untouched.** The flashlight setting itself is left as it is, and the beam is
  back once play resumes (`src/newer/render/r_flashlight.js`).

### Evidence

- **`tests/gl_portal_test.js`.** A teleporter face on a static wall gets its window, and an
  always-on teleporter takes it over a nearer named one. Removing either change fails it.
- **`tests/gl_rsurf_brush_test.js`.** E1M3's `*98` teleporter face is drawn in its entity's
  group, and its mesh and geometry survive the group's rebuild (a dispose listener checks).
  Removing the drawing or the dispose guard fails it.
- **`tests/flashlight_cutscene_test.js`.** The beam is on in play, out in each of the three
  intermission states with the setting untouched, and back after. Removing the check fails it.
- **Browser, end.bsp.**
  - Before the change, the secret teleporter's face was missing (the wall behind it showed).
    After, its window is drawn (`activeFrame` live) and shows the dark room by t67 with its
    torch.
  - Through Shub's own death (`finale_1`), `cl.intermission` was 2 and the beam was out, with
    `r_flashlight` still 1.

## Evidence

- **`tests/end_level_native_test.js`.** The real end.bsp is read from the owner's local registered pak, with the stock
  progs over it as the browser runs the full game; the test is a named skip without the pak.

    a lap at its speed with no jump larger than one frame's travel, and passes through Shub.

    stops, and keeps stock Quake's stops.

  - In both modes, the final teleporter's own `teleport_touch`, while the ball is inside Shub, telefrags him.
