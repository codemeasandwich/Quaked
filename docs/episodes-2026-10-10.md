# The episodes: Dimension of the Past plays, Dimension of the Machine not yet (card [34c]), 10 October 2026

## What works

**Dimension of the Past** (MachineGames, 2016; `dopa`) can be played. Its box on the game shelf is no longer dimmed,
and it opens at `index.html?game=dopa` (or with `game dopa`).

- **How it mounts.** It runs over Quake, like the mission packs. Its own `pak0.pak` (from `games/Dimension of the
  Past/` or `resources/dopa/`) is mounted last, over Quake and the shareware.
- **No switch.** An episode runs as standard Quake: it sets no `-hipnotic`/`-rogue` switch, so the status bar,
  protocol and give command are Quake's own.
- **Saves and settings.** It keeps its own saves (`quake_save_dopa_`) and settings (`quake_config_dopa`, starting from
  Quake's on its first run), as the mission packs do.
- **Its maps.** All 13 are BSP2, which the engine already loads (gl_model.js follows QuakeSpasm's BSP2 layout). Its
  QuakeC uses only built-ins up to #78.
- **Level Select** offers its own maps, by the game running (`GameSelection_Running`), under the names each map gives
  itself in its worldspawn message:
  - the introduction (`start`);
  - E5 (e5m1 to e5m7);
  - the secret levels (e5sm1, e5sm2);
  - the final level (e5end);
  - the deathmatch arena (e5dm).

**Dimension of the Machine** (2021; `mg1`) is not playable yet. The catalogue says why: "some of its levels need more
than 256 models, which needs the FitzQuake protocol (not yet built)".

## The engine limits raised

Dimension of the Machine's start map makes 203 static entities and its hub 485, against WinQuake's limit of 128
(`Host_Error: Too many static entities`). The limits are now raised as later engines raised them:

| Limit | WinQuake | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `MAX_STATIC_ENTITIES` | 128 | 4096 (QuakeSpasm's) |
| `MAX_EFRAGS` | 640 | 16384 |

An efrag is a small object, and the pool is re-chained once per level (`CL_ClearState`). With the new limits, mg1's
`start` and `hub` load and play.

## What stops Dimension of the Machine

Some of its levels precache more than 256 models: `PF_precache_model: overflow`, then `Host_Error: Program error`.
Protocol 15 sends model numbers as single bytes, so more than 256 cannot be named.

- Its first episode (mge1m1 to mge1m3) loads, as do `start` and `hub`.
- mge2m1 stops there, with 255 models already precached.
- After that overflow the page could load no other map: `Mod_ForName: NULL name`, an exception in the page. So the
  later levels were not tried one by one in this probe. (mge5m2 also stopped at the limit when loaded on its own,
  earlier.)
- The page's state after such an overflow is a robustness gap, recorded with the protocol work.

Playing it needs the FitzQuake protocol (666), which sends model and sound numbers as 16 bits, on both the server and
the client. That is the next increment.

## Checks

- **Browser, `?game=dopa`:** all 12 of its playable maps loaded and played (start, e5m1 to e5m7, e5sm1, e5sm2, e5end
  and e5dm), with `impulse 9` and no engine or page errors.
- **Browser, mg1 over Quake (a runtime mount):**
  - before the limits were raised, `start` and `hub` failed with too many static entities;
  - after, both load, with 203 and 485 static entities;
  - mge1m1, mge1m2 and mge1m3 load;
  - mge2m1 (and mge5m2, on its own) stop at 256 models.
- `tests/game_catalogue_test.js`: Dimension of the Past is playable when whole on Quake. Dimension of the Machine is
  validated but not playable, with its reason.
- `tests/game_selection_test.js` and `tests/game_shelf_test.js`: `dopa` can be chosen and opened by URL. `mg1` cannot.
- `tests/mission_pack_test.js`: Level Select under Dimension of the Past offers its own episodes and maps.
