# Glass at the 4x standard

The owner's texture standard (13 Oct 2026): every high-resolution texture is four times its original
size, on the web and in the app alike. The glass materials (windows and stained glass in every game,
`newer/textures/glass/`) were the last set still at their earlier size.

## What changed

- 86 glass variants have new 4x images: diffuse, normal, gloss and height for each (344 files, named
  `<hash>-4x-<role>.png`), made by `tools/glass_materials/stage_fourfold.py` from the same reviewed
  sources and pane regions as before (`tools/glass_materials/regions.json`, `apply_regions.py`).
- `newer/textures/index.json`'s `glass` section points at them (its `version` is 2026101402, so a
  client fetches the new index). The earlier images stay in the folder for the records that name them.
- `glass/provenance.json`, `region-proof.json` and `region-review.json` record each new image's hash
  and its assembled normal. The install record is
  `tools/texture_sheets/sources/official-world-2026-10-12/glass-fourfold-record/`.
- Normal maps for the new images are made at load (`R_GenerateNormalData`), as for any texture
  without a prepared bake; no prepared bakes were added for them.

## Checks

`tests/glass_region_assets_test.js` decodes every one of the 130 reviewed glass materials from the
actual new PNGs and checks the assembled normal and height against the recorded proof, and that each
of the 58 reviewed panes keeps its aperture inside the pane. `tests/glass_materials_test.js` and the
texture suites pass with the new index. Not yet seen: the 4x glass in a browser.
