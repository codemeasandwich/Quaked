## Reused artwork and composition

The owner's `player-face-layers-v4.1.0.html` supplies the artwork and its reviewed registration. Its embedded PNGs were decoded byte-for-byte into `newer/hud/playerface/`; the supplied HTML was not modified. `SOURCE.json` records the donor SHA-256 and every embedded image's SHA-256. The manifest and canvas operations preserve full 96×96 origins, fitted transparent rasters, clipping and nearest sampling. No replacement head artwork was generated.

`r_playerface.js` composes **base head → expression → gaze → invisibility mask → injury decal → powered eyes**. Three canonical heads support five ordered looks, with frontal expression/blood/mask reused for eye-only glances. It selects one complete fitted injury decal for the current stage; those decals already contain each stage's accumulated wound pattern. Drawing every previous stage again would incorrectly darken/obscure the face. The same head remains underneath.

Health bands match the donor: H1=100–91%, H2=90–81%, continuing in ten-point bands to H9=20–11%, H10=10–0%. Health above100 is visually clamped to100. Injuries remain independent of expression, gaze and power. This health-based HUD damage is separate from the water-only gun/body coating implemented earlier; healing may select a healthier HUD stage.

Quad uses purple eyes and purple wisps. Invulnerability uses gold/yellow cores and glow without yellow flames. Both together use gold cores with purple wisps. Invisibility blacks out normal skin/features while preserving the metal frame, injury layer and powered eyes above it. All eight combinations use the donor's registered eye positions. Effects here are the supplied transparent HUD layers; the world power-up flame shaders are a separate system.

The portrait uses the existing native24×24 HUD location, with the 96px source sampled without smoothing. Newer Game and its HUD option gate the new face. Classic, the Classic demo half and HUD-off retain their original portrait priorities. Missing selected layers retain the native WAD fallback. HUD preload starts the face batch alongside the existing sprite batch behind the console, reports readiness/fallback, bounds each manifest/image request to30seconds, and ignores late callbacks after failure.

## Independent gameplay variables

`face_state.js` owns transient attention, expression and life timers independently of drawing or artwork. HUD-left means the player's left, not anatomical left. Ordered looks are `head_left`, `eyes_left`, `front`, `eyes_right`, `head_right`. Normal transitions take roughly80ms per neighbor, impact transitions40ms. Even a delayed rendered frame crosses only one neighboring pose; no crossfade or stretched intermediate head is generated.

Idle uses only left/right eye glances, randomized1.5–3seconds apart and held0.3–0.6seconds. Movement, strafing and mouse turns never select a portrait direction. Directional hits use the view at impact: ±15° stays centered,15–45° uses an eye glance,45–165° uses a full head turn, and within15° of directly behind is centered. Unknown/world/fall/floor-trigger damage is centered. A reaction lasts approximately1second; repeated same-direction hits refresh the deadline without restarting traversal. Damage totals over the preceding200ms select the winning pose, with the latest hit breaking ties. Raw native impact times drive arbitration even when packet delivery intervals differ.

Direction priority is **death → hit reaction → forward attack → idle**. (Since 9 October 2026 an off-screen enemy that has just noticed the player also takes the hit-reaction slot, a one-second glance toward it with no pain: [face-alert-2026-10-09.md](face-alert-2026-10-09.md).) Expression priority is **death → shock → pain → grin → focus → normal**. Actual health loss strictly above20 in one damage call causes1second of shock; lesser hits cause0.2seconds of pain and do not refresh an existing heavy shock. Pain/shock and current injury apply immediately while the head finishes turning.

Actual sustained firing/cycling for2seconds produces focus; focus retains a0.3second tail after attacking stops. Merely holding an empty/unusable weapon is insufficient. Firing centers attention unless an impact overrides it, and idle resumes only after at least0.5seconds. Reward grin lasts2seconds for a newly acquired currently-unowned weapon, a successful major power-up pickup (including active-power refresh, and the native envirosuit), or an actual secret message. Ammo, small health gains and routine kill statistics do not grin. Rewards/powers never recenter the head. Interrupted expression deadlines keep counting down; old native rewards do not replay after a hidden HUD.

The donor has no separate death art, so death freezes the selected pose with the supplied shocked expression and H10 injury. The health/clientdata boundary resets to forward/normal on dead→alive, including when the HUD/console prevents portrait sampling. Native event queues are cleared at that reset; an old lethal event cannot reappear in the new life. Map/clock changes reset transient controller state. Nothing changes player movement, health, ammunition, inventory, power durations or respawn physics.

## Event boundaries and failure handling

`r_facegame.js` adapts actual `V_ParseDamage`, clientdata item/health updates and secret messages. `sv_faceevents.js` observes stock native QC function entry/leave through the existing interpreter hook architecture without mutating gameplay. It requires stockCRC24778, a paired local loopback, active single-player Newer Game, and a living local player. Demo, remote, dedicated and multiplayer state cannot leak local-server events.

Each native `T_Damage` records HP before/after separately, including hits separated by healing, armor loss, source position and impact view angle. This avoids deriving heavy shock from a net health delta or an aggregated damage packet. Native authoritative empty drains do not fall back to the aggregate and duplicate a reaction. Confirmed ammo consumption identifies ranged discharge; axe animation starts identify swings. Nested super-shotgun/super-nail calls are deduplicated. Dry auto-switch, release and unusable attack do not invent shots. Successful physical `powerup_touch` observes native timer/items/trigger changes, including collecting an already-active Quad again.

Queues are bounded256records per kind and invalidated on program/world/map changes or interpreter failure. Captured times are retained for impact arbitration; display deadlines map the event's actual server age onto client game time, with separate receipt time so an old event cannot rewind the display controller. The bridge is observational and preserves the pre-existing axe/respawn callbacks and their ordering.

Remote/demo/custom-QC protocols lack these native per-call observations. They retain a client packet/weapon-animation fallback: source position and impact-time client view supply direction, damage bytes supply health loss, and changing nonzero weapon cycles supply firing evidence. The existing protocol does not identify an unknown inflictor or repeat an already-set power bit. Exact single-call loss/unknown-source/repeated-active-pickup guarantees are therefore qualified for stock local single-player, not asserted for arbitrary remote mods. Demo face state is isolated from live play.
