# ttex054 physical-feature survey and existing-layered-policy decision

Status: **HOLD before physical-geometry approval**. No layered master, canonical recipe, tiers or new producer has been authored. The preceding interpolation failures remain retained.

The unchanged `prepare_layered_texture_master.compose_layered` can reuse explicit polygon masks, a common upper face, a negative substrate floor and a facing-side Gaussian bevel. With `microAmplitude:0`, RGB contributes no relief at all. The field is the common face outside those explicit recesses. The production CLI still needs the independent initial source/master replay proof; this investigation does not bypass its guard or fabricate that proof.

## Actual source survey

`physical-survey-v1/` retains whole-source coordinate grids for all four green plaster quadrants, both red bands, and both halves of the lower red field. The earlier actual meander/source-corner grids were also inspected. Ninety-five enlarged source crops cover the visible aggregate/flake structures in the green field and pitting in both red bands. They are source pixels, not generated examples or a darkness-derived semantic mask.

The preliminary manual classification is 51 raised/mixed flake or granular-aggregate crops, 32 unresolved fine-relief/colour crops, and 12 proposed negative-cavity/chip-void candidates. Those labels are hypotheses requiring independent review. `proposed-cavity-polygon-contact.png` then falsified several initial candidates: some nominal polygons miss the actual feature or include a raised speck. The first polygon pass is explicitly **not accepted geometry** and is retained as failed annotation evidence. It must not be copied to a canonical recipe.

The few stronger cavity hypotheses are S015 (small green pore), S020 (narrow chipped void/undercut), S032 (green cavity beneath chipped material), S093 (round dark pit in the middle red band) and S094 (small red-band pore). Their exact sign and boundary remain reviewable source interpretations. S037/S041/S045/S054 and parts of S074/S078/S089 require location or physical-sign rejection/refinement; darkness alone is not sufficient. In particular, the initial S041 polygon missed the small dark site and S074 includes a pale raised speck. No negative floor is approved for them.

## P1–P4 and lower-field semantics

- **P1:** a lifted plaster flake and adjacent occluded edge. Its bright face is raised/mixed geometry, not a negative pore box. Carving the whole witness would invert its physical meaning. A dark undercut alone does not recover the flake's positive relief.
- **P2:** mixed granular aggregate with interspersed small cavities, including the S015 candidate. Individual cavities can be proposed; the whole aggregate patch cannot be a floor.
- **P3:** pale worn aggregate/flake cluster with a possible adjacent cavity (S032). The pale raised cluster and cavity require distinct semantics. It is not all negative.
- **P4:** red pitted/granular plaster. Most fine dark marks and bright grains cannot be confidently assigned a common negative floor from this image alone. The first candidate inside this region is not an approved pore polygon.
- **Lower red field:** its dense red/dark islands remain coating-loss, pigment and illumination confounded. Selecting every dark island as a pore would recreate colour-derived embossing. No reliable negative pore network was identified there, so this route leaves the entire field on the common face.

## Why the existing two-plane route is not yet adequate

The source's convincing plaster structure is mainly mixed raised flakes and fine aggregate. The current negative-only two-plane policy can depict bounded chipped cavities, but cannot reconstruct positive flake/aggregate relief above the common face. Setting microAmplitude to zero also removes every unannotated pore/grain signal. A handful of the stronger cavity candidates would therefore produce an almost entirely planar height field, with the entire lower third and most red/ochre material flat. That would be sparse illustrative pits, not demonstrated convincing plaster roughness at High or Mid. This limitation cannot be waived by the unchanged photographic RGB or by labelling the planes physical.

The cavity candidates are mostly several master pixels across. Mid halves those dimensions before map encoding, so several become one-to-three pixels; the small uncertain candidates would be especially fragile. The 256-reference bevel also scales by `sqrt(512*1024)/256 = 2.828427` at this master. Its physical smoothing width must be chosen from these actual small features, not copied from large chipped-brick recipes. No production depth/bevel value has been selected merely to make a sparse map visible.

Root independently reviews the actual source survey and proposed signs/boundaries first. An unchanged-policy diagnostic can illustrate a reviewed set if useful, but no convincing whole-material relief claim is justified by the current supported negative-only subset. If the existing policy cannot represent the source's mixed physical structure without an invented pore network, this route remains HOLD with this specific semantic/coverage cause. It grants no permission for new generated detail, per-stroke albedo fits, procedural grain, donor clones, new producer or altered material gate.
