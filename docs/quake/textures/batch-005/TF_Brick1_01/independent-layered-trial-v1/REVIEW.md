## Why physical ART remains HOLD

I viewed the actual full 1024 height contrast display, UV XYZ diagnostic, both neutral opposed lights, both colour-lit views, flat control, literal X/Y/corner crops, and two source/mask/height/XYZ/light focus panels. The colour image remains convincing photographic brick, but the neutral response shows narrow, unusually straight grooves with rectangular ledges. The low groove follows the conservative core, while substantial visibly green mortar outside it rises toward the broad face domain. Its physical transition follows the narrow polygon boundary rather than the chipped, irregular brick/mortar interface visible in RGB.

This is clearest in [the independently preclassified horizontal contact](own-preclassified-c0-c1-shoulders-six-panel-nearest4.png): the source's wider uneven mortar is represented by a narrow dark channel and a broad adjacent elevated shoulder. Both light directions reveal the same straight transition with reversed light/dark sides. [The course14 contact panel](c14-j3-source-contact-six-panel-nearest4.png) shows the corresponding vertical behavior. That second location was examined after the author's separate shoulder warning; it is supporting visual context, not a newly blinded witness or the basis of an invented independent pre-scalar claim. The independently sealed course0/course1 roles and full-frame neutral views already expose the problem.

The height display is contrast-normalized for readability only. The normal and light images use the actual, unscaled float amplitudes, so the finding is not based solely on the contrast display. The opposite-light and flat views separate the relief response from albedo. This is an offline physical ART judgment; no hardware shader, glancing-angle renderer or POM observation is claimed.

Literal X/Y/corner views retain the intended course intersections without a new full perimeter strip. That bounded wrap observation cannot clear the internal mortar/arris finding. The previous independent V4 PASS accepted the RGB placement of conservative contact cores and explicitly excluded complete outer arris segmentation and actual shoulder response. The present actual trial exposes that remaining gate; its findings do not rewrite the earlier limited result.
