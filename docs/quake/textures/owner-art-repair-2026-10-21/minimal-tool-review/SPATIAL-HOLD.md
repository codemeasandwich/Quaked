# Spatial proposal after actual base-wall and hardware review

The proposed whole-strip tech10_3 Y landmark trial is **HOLD before issuance**. Do not execute the earlier louver-only coordinate proposal. The [literal annotated comparison](tech10_3-hardware-louver-conflict.png) shows ten original left-band ring instances versus nine rounded heads in the retained owner artwork. The louver band and hardware have different spacing errors, so a single full-width Y fit cannot preserve both relationships. This finding comes from the actual visible repeated forms; it does not infer physical relief from their colour.

The source-bound [audit](tech10_3-spatial-HOLD.json) pins native 32×128, both retained lossless 128×512 diffuse/height PNGs, their historical producer hashes, current height descriptor and independently exact no-op replays for both roles. Current normal strength is 1.15; the descriptor omits `cap`. Preserve that exact descriptor rather than guessing another profile or adding a cap.

The earlier progressive-louver finding remains real. Native louver gap troughs at 28.5, 41.5, 54.5, 67.5, 80.5, 93.5 and 106.5 native texels correspond to owner troughs about 28.875, 41.875, 54.875, 67.875, 80.375, 93.125 and 105.875. A uniform phase cannot remove this changing error. Earlier bright-crest coordinates were less reliable because a rail's rivet highlights and flange are different subfeatures; they were never sealed as an executable recipe. Use the orange trough lines in the annotation to distinguish that measurement from an accepted geometric centre.

The left-band native repetition is about 12 native texels, while the owner has nine rounded heads at roughly 13.3-native-texel intervals. A monotone full-strip Y remap cannot create a tenth instance. More immediately, the second head is approximately aligned, while several middle owner heads sit below the corresponding native rings. The louver-driven map would move some of those heads farther down, worsening an unrelated part of the artwork. Subpixel photometric shadow minima have about one native texel of visual-centre uncertainty; this does not explain the repeated-form count difference.

The full base-wall comparison also confirms that tech08_4 is a different current authoring lineage with louvers that already track the original more closely. Do not warp the whole neighboring family together. The scene lane separately identified an extra top rail/clipped hardware in owner tech08_1: an endpoint-preserving monotone warp cannot remove an extra feature, and a global phase would disturb its other already near-aligned forms. No tech08_1 warp is proposed here.

For any future authorized **global** spatial trial, the smallest existing mechanism remains the unchanged finisher, with `--factor 4 --method photographic --comparison-divisor 2 --color-strength 0`, the same sealed `--vertical-landmarks` file for diffuse and retained RGB height, no edge anchor, and two new PNG outputs. At output row j, its sampler maps `(j+.5)/4` through native→donor points, multiplies by four, subtracts .5, then linearly interpolates the two wrapped donor rows and rounds/clips to uint8. Applying that same sampler to all retained height channels preserves registration and the existing depth profile policy; it does not recreate full-precision scalar relief. Recomputing height from warped/colour-corrected brightness is not acceptable.

There is no existing full-strip parameter recipe that resolves the demonstrated competing constraints. The present supported increment is the three-file colour-only trial, with the spatial finding retained. A region-specific warp would require a separate reviewed implementation choice and could introduce an internal discontinuity across the plate/louver interface; no x-masked solver, compositor or new tool is proposed or authored in this lane. Root should resolve the artwork-preservation tradeoff against the actual scene before changing geometry. No phase, landmark file, corrected diffuse or height was issued by this reviewer.
