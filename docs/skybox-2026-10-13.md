# Skyboxes (13 October 2026)

The skybox half of the owner's "skyboxes and fog" card (dadcb966; fog: [fog-2026-10-13.md](fog-2026-10-13.md)).
Arcane Dimensions (32 of its maps) and Quoth name skyboxes in their maps; this port drew their
classic scrolling sky instead ("'sky' is not a field" was the only sign).

## What it does

- **`src/engine/render/r_skybox.js`**, as Quakespasm and FitzQuake:
  - a map's worldspawn `sky` key (or `skyname`; a leading `_` allowed, as Dimension of the Machine's
    `_sky`; or `qlsky`, with `_` added) names six images, `gfx/env/<name>rt`, `bk`, `lf`, `ft`, `up`
    and `dn` (`.tga`); the `sky` console command changes it (`sky` alone prints it, `sky ""` returns
    to the scrolling sky); a map naming the same skybox as the last keeps it without decoding again;
  - the images are decoded here (`R_DecodeTGA`: uncompressed or run-length, 24 or 32 bits, or 8-bit
    grey, either row order; a header asking for more than 4096 × 4096, or an uncompressed file
    shorter than its pixels, is refused before anything is made); a missing face shows dark grey, as
    Quakespasm keeps a skybox with a missing face; with no face found the map keeps its scrolling sky;
  - the six sides are laid out as Quakespasm lays them (its `st_to_vec` and `skytexorder`, from
    Quake II): right +x, left -x, back +y, front -y, up +z, down -z (`R_SkyBoxSide`).
- **Drawing**: the map's own sky surfaces draw the skybox (`gl_rsurf_liquids.js`): their material
  looks up the six images by the direction from the camera to each point (`R_SkyBoxMaterial`; the GLSL
  is generated from the same table `R_SkyBoxDirection` is tested by), so every view that draws the
  world (teleporter windows, water reflections, each eye in XR) sees the sky around its own camera,
  and the sky keeps its depth rule, its 1.9 glow and the fog's share. The scrolling cloud layer is
  left out under a skybox.
- **Not Quakespasm's / not yet**: the Newer Game picture's sun colour and light shafts are still
  made from the map's classic sky texture, not from the skybox's images; there is no `skyfog`
  worldspawn key or `r_skyfog` setting.

## Checks

- `tests/skybox_test.js`: TGA in both row orders, run-length runs and raw packets across rows, 32-bit
  alpha, 8-bit grey, an ID field, refused colour-mapped, cut-off and oversized files; the side layout
  (each side's image, every corner's direction found again by the lookup, the shader's +x side);
  worldspawn `sky`, `_sky`, `qlsky`, `skyname`, a partial set, a missing set, the same skybox kept,
  the `sky` command; the material's shader (world position, camera, fog share, six images) and the
  renderer's calls in place. `tests/sv_large_protocol_native_test.js` adds `sv_protocol 999`.
- In the app page (WebKit, classic picture): Quoth's `start` (`sky` "dusk") shows its dusk skybox in
  the open ceiling with the walls whole around it.
- Not yet seen: a skybox in the Newer Game picture in a browser (Newer Game stalls while loading in
  headless WebKit on this machine). The faces' orientation follows Quakespasm's tables (checked
  against them by review); whether every edge meets as the artist made it has been judged only from
  Quoth's dusk sky.
