# Sparks at ambient lights

Effect 7 of T-3faa98e3 reuses the ordinary `ambience/buzz1.wav` static-sound cue used by sparking
fixtures. Every game uses the same cue and the same existing sound parser/registry; no map list or
new audio playback is introduced. Options → Newer Game features → **Light sparks** (`r_sparks`,
archived, default 1) controls bounded, short falling bursts. Classic excludes them; paused-off removes
existing sparks immediately; world/mode/clock resets restart schedules.

The shared mote layer owns one 128-slot fixture-spark reservation. Its existing premultiplied/additive
shader and distance fog draw the light; no new material or mesh is made. Motion uses the existing
`SV_RecursiveHullCheck` through `FX_Blocked`, checking start-solid/all-solid/fraction rather than the
recursive routine's Boolean result. The callback is attached to owner `sparks`, not kind `MOTE_SPARK`:
future nail impacts may share the kind without inheriting fixture collision or switch behavior.

Each live spark traces its previous position to the proposed one before it can be drawn, at most 128
queries per advancing frame for sparks (nail impacts and embers trace their own reservations the
same way, so the layer as a whole makes up to 384). A thin wall between two empty endpoints is still hit. There is no cached
source-floor termination plane: a spark crossing a ledge falls until its actual world collision or
expiry. Actual spawn positions must be open, and new-source validity checks are bounded to eight per
frame. Moving doors/lifts are outside the world-hull query, as documented for the shared helpers.

Fixture sparks have their own state in the existing `FX_Random` generator. The default drip stream
keeps its original sequence exactly, so adding or toggling sparks does not change accepted drip
schedules, drops or splashes. No accepted effect's particle or light budget is borrowed.

An extra dynamic light was deliberately omitted: the existing dynamic-light allocator and post-light
selector can evict an accepted weapon or fixture light when full. The shared additive glow provides
the spark effect without crossing that preservation boundary.

Public tests cover thin-wall/floor/ceiling/start-solid collision, missing geometry, ledge traversal,
owner isolation, emission, paused-off retirement and identical seeded drip snapshots with sparks on
and off; after the independent review of 14 Oct also the timing (5 to 20 bursts of 4 to 9 sparks in 30 s
from one light), the 900-unit reach, a light set inside a wall, eight new lights checked a frame, and no
burst of every light at once when the switch is turned back on (`tests/sparks_test.js`, 9 tests).

**Switching back on**: each light's next burst is spread out again over the next six seconds.

**Where the cue comes from**: `ambience/buzz1.wav` is placed by Quake's `light_fluorospark` and also by
the sound-only `ambient_flouro_buzz`, so a map may show sparks where it plays the buzz without a
visible fitting.

Before this commit, the earlier session's independent run (its probes are not kept in the repository)
passed 24 checks; its additional probes covered the real buzz static-sound packet, rewinds, invalid lowered spawns,
sloped traces, 1,024 preserved drip RNG samples, full reservation and the actual paused Options toggle.
`tests/sparks_gpu_trial.html` passed five WebKit GPU checks with no errors: visible front-of-wall light,
retirement at a thin wall, depth occlusion of a still-live spark, a positive visibility control with the
wall hidden, and paused-off invisibility. This is synthetic geometry/material proof; native play
acceptance and the scoped commit/release gate remain separate.
