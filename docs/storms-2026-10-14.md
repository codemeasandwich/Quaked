# Living skies: lightning with the thunder, gusts out under the sky

Item 5 of the add-on effects for every game (card T-3faa98e3; plan:
[addon-effects-integration-2026-10-13.md](addon-effects-integration-2026-10-13.md)), as Arcane
Dimensions, Soul of Evil, Abyss of Pandemonium, X-Men and Scourge of Armagon have them.

## What it does (Newer Game, every supported game)

- **Lightning with the thunder.** Whenever a game plays a thunder sound (any sound whose name has
  "thunder" in it: Scourge's random thunder, the add-ons' storms), the sky flashes: three quick
  flickers over a third of a second, brighter the louder and nearer the thunder is (by Quake's own
  attenuation, so thunder too far off to hear does not flash; the player's own thunder and thunder
  placed nowhere, as the mixer hears them, at full), and in Newer Game's lighting the sun's light on
  open ground flashes with it. For the eyes' sake there is never more than one flash a second (thunder
  closer together is one flash, the stronger), so never more than three flickers a second, and the
  brightest is 2.2 times the sky. Paused, the sky is as always: a flash is never held. The flash comes with the sound's start; each game's thunder
  cracks a few tenths of a second into its sound, so the boom follows the flash as it would.
- **Gusts.** Out under the sky, where the wind ambient already plays, a gust passes every 9 to 26
  seconds: the game's own gust sounds where it has them (Arcane Dimensions' `ambience/windgust1..6.wav`),
  else Quake's `ambience/windfly.wav`; quietly, from one side, as loud as the wind is there, and only
  while more than four sound channels are free (`S_StartSoundIfFree`'s reserve, `snd_dma.js`), so the
  game's own sounds always find a free channel and none is ever cut off.

## What decides where it happens

The storm is the game's: thunder and its lightning only happen where a game or map plays thunder.
Stock Quake plays none, so its maps get gusts outdoors but no lightning. (Adding distant thunder of
our own to every outdoor stock map would change those maps' mood; it is not done, and is a question
for the owner.)

## What it leaves alone

The sky's fog share, the skybox, cloud shadows and the scrolling sky are unchanged: the flash only
brightens the sky for its moment (`R_SkyFlashSet` in `r_fog.js`, applied in the same sky material
patch before the fog share). The wind ambient channel is unchanged; gusts are separate sounds. Off
in New Game, in demos, in the classic half of the title demo and with `r_storm 0` (saved; Options,
Newer features, "Storms and gusts"). The storm has its own random numbers (`FX_Random( 'storm' )`),
so drips, sparks, nails and embers keep theirs.

## Code

- `src/newer/render/r_storm.js`: `R_StormSound` (told of each sound as it starts, through
  `S_AddSoundStarted` in `snd_dma.js`, which tells its listeners only once a game sound is playing on its
  channel, and skips a listener that fails), `R_StormFrame` (each frame, from `gl_rmain.js`),
  `R_StormFlashNow` (read by the Newer sun, `r_post_sky.js`), `R_StormClear` (each new map).
- `src/engine/render/r_fog.js`: the sky materials' shared flash value.

## Checks

`tests/storm_test.js`, through the real sound system with the shareware pack's sounds and e1m1's
leaves: a thunder sound flashes at once, flickers, is over within 0.3 s and is given to the sky's
material, whose fog share is unchanged; quieter thunder is dimmer; thunder too far to hear and other
sounds do not flash; New Game, a demo, the switch off and the classic half do not flash; outdoors a
minute brings 2 to 7 gusts, indoors and paused none; a game's own gust sound is used first; the
drips' random numbers are untouched; thunder 0.3 s after thunder adds no flickers; a pause holds no
flash; the sun reads the same flash as the sky; the player's own thunder flashes fully. Not yet seen or heard in a browser.
