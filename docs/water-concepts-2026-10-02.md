## Changes and contracts

- Above-water reflection retains normal reflectance 0.02 and gain 1.65, with artistic grazing exponent 2.5. Normal-incidence transmission remains dominant. The existing reflection strength/off controls remain authoritative.
- Clear original texture coverage is 0.05, Tinted 0.08 and Muddy 0.10. Muddy absorption becomes `[0.035, 0.055, 0.080]`, suppressing recognizable submerged floor detail without relying on the old painted surface. Original textures, UVs, material caches and map physics remain authoritative. Toxic coverage remains 0.22.
- Ripple slopes have one dominant component and smaller crossing/fine components. Existing profile amplitude/speed and pixel-footprint filtering remain. Reflection, refraction and flashlight glints share the same normal.
- Confirmed SSR hits now shade ordinary opaque receivers using the primary receiver's point-light falloff, material floor, additive contribution and shadow policy. The eight selected candidates are ranked by their contribution at that hit; only the strongest three receive eight-step visibility tests. This happens once after a successful ray hit, not on every march step. Main point lighting reuses the same extracted helpers.
- Reflection guards preserve lighting-off, sky, portal sentinels, invalid albedo and the viewmodel depth range. Mapped normals must agree with opaque depth as they do in primary lighting; stale normals behind transparent overlays fall back to depth normals. Flashlight-only misses retain an early cone exit.
- Stock brown texture `*04water1` selects Muddy when Water appearance is **Map**. Actual E1M3 contains this ordinary water texture; it is not slime. Its three regions retain physical kind 0. Material and composite use the same default, and regions with different authored optics do not merge. Explicit Clear/Tinted/Muddy/Toxic water choices override this default. Actual slime always retains its Toxic profile; lava and teleporters retain separate handling. Classic/New Game/liquids-off preserve native materials and behavior.
- Muddy above-water views gain a low 18-unit scattering layer, bounded to pool XY, clipped to opaque depth, excluded at portal sentinels and capped to 240 units of ray travel. It reuses the strongest of the existing eight incident sources with one eight-step shadow query. This incident light also supplies brown body scattering when the underlying floor is dark. The upward-facing source estimate is an artistic proxy, not full isotropic volume transport.
- The existing **Surface haze** slider (`r_mist`, formerly labelled Toxic mist) controls this small air layer as well as existing toxic wisps. At zero the Muddy air haze disappears; incident-light body scattering remains. Brown scattering has no emission and produces no source-free glow. Toxic's additive particle system remains separate. It now uses four staggered wisps in each narrow anchored stream: smaller 48-unit sprites, 60–100-unit rise and slow 20–33-second cycles replace the larger loose cloud. Sway is limited to approximately 3–11 units, colour strength is lower and the per-pool cap falls from 170 to 120 wisps, with the existing 720 total cap. Geometry/depth testing, cached texture/material and native/classic gates are retained. Underwater views skip the air layer, preserving the Snell window and total internal reflection.

No extra render target/pass, probe recapture frequency, geometry displacement, foam or new physics was added. Captures retain two cached 256-pixel cubes, six faces per capture and the existing rate limit. Off-screen cube radiance remains pre-deferred; enhanced torch/flashlight relighting is supplied only for valid on-screen opaque SSR hits. Screen-space shadow/reflection and merged-volume limitations remain.
