Newer Game wall textures: upscaled versions of the original Quake textures, made by the project owner with an AI upscaler from sheets cut by tools/texture_sheets, then realigned and sliced (realign_sheets.py).

Wizard textures updated from the owner-supplied 2026-10-01 sheet. Source PNG and measured crop/alignment recipe: tools/texture_sheets/sources/wizard-2026-10-01.*. Extracted pixels are resized/aligned to the existing 256x256 layout and losslessly encoded; matching crafted heights are regenerated by tools/craft_normals.py. Dark seam lines the upscaler left at texture edges were repaired in place on 2026-10-02 by tools/texture_sheets/fix_seams.py (recorded in tools/texture_sheets/seam_fixes.json); see docs/texture-seams-2026-10-02.md.
