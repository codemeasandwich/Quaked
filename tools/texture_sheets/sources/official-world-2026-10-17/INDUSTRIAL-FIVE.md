# Industrial material continuation

The three narrow horizontal trims metal5_90_JM, wmet4_25_JM and wmet4_27_JM each contain two full-width courses. The original 128×16/32 aspect must be retained as 512×64/128. The supporting square image-generation guide repeats one original strip vertically eight or four times; it is one repeated material, not an atlas of unrelated textures. `crop_repeated_strip.py` extracts an explicit measured middle pair of photographed courses before the standard source fit. The measured raw bounds and hashes are retained. Original lip and dark-seam phase, rather than an invented count of vertical ribs, determines the layout.

TF_Metal4_6d has a 16×16 grid at eight native texels per stud. A single GPT photograph supplies the real domed iron motif. Its first fitted 32px motif retained the count but made the heads too small, so independent review held it. The selected repair crops the original 1254-square donor to the central 896-square region before reduction; it does not enlarge an already 32px image. `tile_stud_motif.py` repeats the reviewed 32px motif at the original pitch, then the full source-specific colour field preserves the native subdued brown variation. No procedural noise or new geometry is introduced. Held inputs remain intact.

TF_MetWall2_02 retains two broad horizontal blue-grey courses with no vertical centre joint. Its photographic diffuse passed, but default 0.2 relief made the quiet painted surface look pebbled. The selected version uses strength 0.06 and cap 0.5. Diffuse and height bytes are unchanged; X/Y/Z maps are regenerated from the decoded quality 92 height at that strength. Independent two-light CPU review confirms shallow painted-steel relief. Actual engine review remains a separate check.

All runtime diffuse, height and grayscale X/Y/Z images use lossy WebP quality 92 and exact 4× native dimensions. The existing native-name/pixel guard, Classic fallback, native UVs, companion-pack routing and normal loader are retained. Half-native and quarter-native comparisons, decoded full-size material/feature-scale review, source-phase repeats, actual encoder replay and actual GPU review precede final independent review and the normal maintained commit gate. Dev publication does not mean owner appearance acceptance or release.
