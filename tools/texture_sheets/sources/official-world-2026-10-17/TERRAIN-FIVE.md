# Terrain material continuation

Five original Dimension of the Machine terrain sources receive photographic replacements: TF_Grass1, TF_Grass2, TF_Ground1, TF_Cliff1 and TF_Cliff1b. The four 256-square sources become 1024-square; Ground1's 128-square source becomes 512-square. Every runtime diffuse, height and grayscale X/Y/Z map uses lossy WebP quality 92. Primary and secondary comparisons reduce the actual original and decoded replacement to half and quarter the native dimensions.

Grass1 keeps its brighter yellow-olive groundcover. Grass2 and Ground1 retain darker muted olive-brown earth. Their source images are related, but not pixel-identical; each has a distinct source guard, photograph and colour fit. Small coherent moss/grass/root forms, soil crumbs and sparse tiny weathering details replace native grain without adding prominent objects.

Cliff1 preserves the original ground/moss cap in the upper 32 native rows, transition into exposed rock by 64, and darker lower rock around 192..256. Cliff1b remains exposed fractured rock. The large shelves and fracture layout remain recognisably similar; small photographic cracks and chips are artistic interpretation, not exact pixel recovery.

Actual PAK/BSP UV evidence is saved in verification/terrain-five-native-uv-data.json and the independent plan. In the bounded eight-map static-world sample, 100/4626 Cliff1 faces and 735/2832 Cliff1b faces cross a V-repeat boundary. The remaining 19 Cliff1b faces belong to inline/submodels outside that static-world count. Actual shared edges include same-V mid-rock cuts as well as a V224-to-V0 join. This disproves a universal closed two-image vertical cycle. Do not infer paired normal context, or globally clamp/reflect vertical edges, from the texture names alone.

The existing generic periodic normal recipe is retained. Colour finishing preserves Cliff1's intentional cap/base distribution and corrects its horizontal crop join; Cliff1b also receives ordinary vertical colour-join finishing. Native engine UVs and wrap modes are unchanged. Independent decoded height/XYZ review found no artificial strong rim requiring a new normal mechanism. Two shared-UV diagnostic cuts preserve coherent primary shelves despite natural fine-detail differences. These are bounded 2D/CPU checks, not an all-level gameplay qualification.

The actual renderer continues selecting each ordinary replacement by exact source name and native pixel key. Diffuse and authored height/XYZ maps use existing bindings, strength 0.2 and cap 0.5; Classic restores the original image and unknown pixel identities remain native. Public-source checks, encoder replay, companion-pack reachability, actual GPU material review, final independent review and the maintained complete commit gate precede Dev publication. Owner appearance acceptance and release remain separate.
